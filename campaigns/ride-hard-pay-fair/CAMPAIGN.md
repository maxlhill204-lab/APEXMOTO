# APEX MOTO — Ride Hard. Pay Fair.

## Purpose

An evergreen, entertainment-first brand Reel. The film earns attention as a dirt-bike short, then reveals APEX MOTO in the final two seconds.

## Format

- Instagram Reel
- 1080 × 1920, 9:16
- 15 seconds
- 24 fps look
- Original sound design; no voiceover and no copyrighted music

## Brand position

**Tagline:** Ride hard. Pay fair.

**Campaign hook:** The dirt does not care what logo you paid for.

APEX MOTO offers a tight range of straightforward off-road gear at clear prices. Product details and the limits of supplied documentation are published openly. The campaign must never claim laboratory equivalence, matching protection, or matching quality against a named premium helmet without evidence.

## On-screen copy

No text before 11.5 seconds.

1. `THE DIRT DOES NOT CARE WHAT LOGO YOU PAID FOR.`
2. `RIDE HARD. PAY FAIR.`
3. `APEXMOTO.COM.AU`

## Visual rules

- Deep blacks, cream typography and warm gold highlights
- Cool dawn ambience, restrained halation and fine grain
- Realistic off-road motion and suspension behaviour
- One consistent matte-black helmet and black/gold goggle design
- No neon outlines, sci-fi interfaces, floating products or slideshow cards
- No fake manufacturer or safety logos
- No temporary sale price: the film remains usable after a promotion ends

## Export check

- Text remains inside the Reel safe area
- Hook is readable for at least 1.2 seconds
- End card is readable for at least 1.5 seconds
- Peak blacks do not crush all helmet detail
- Sound remains clean on phone speakers and does not clip
- Poster frame uses the helmet beauty shot, not a text-only card
